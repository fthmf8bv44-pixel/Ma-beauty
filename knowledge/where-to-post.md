# Where should this go? (decision guide)

| What you have | Post it as | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Treatment process, result, satisfying clip | **Reel** (share to Facebook too) + **TikTok** (re-upload, don't share the IG link) | Reels/TikTok reach people who don't follow you yet |
| Educational talk-to-camera | **Reel + TikTok**, later a **Meta ad** if it performs | Builds trust and turns viewers into consults |
| A Reel that beat your average views/saves in its first 48 h | **Boost it as a Meta ad** (Ads Manager → use existing post) | Proven content = cheaper results |
| Quick update, availability this week, behind the scenes | **Story** (+ poll/question sticker) | Followers see it, and it reminds warm people to book |
| Client review | **Carousel or Story**, pin best to a **Highlight** | Social proof |
| Before/after (real, consented) | **Organic only** (Reel, carousel, Highlight). **Never in ads** | Meta/TikTok ad policy |
| Content made with an influencer or happy client | **Partnership ad** (see `knowledge/partnership-ads.md`) | Their face + name = trust, often lower cost |
| Strong TikTok that's getting views | **Spark Ad** (boost it in TikTok Ads Manager) | Keeps likes/comments and looks native |
| Offer, price, gift vouchers | **Meta ad + Story + Google Business Profile post** | Direct response |

## Rules of thumb
- Post organically first, and put ad money behind what already works.
- Every Reel also goes to TikTok and Facebook. One shoot, three placements.
- Pin your 3 best Reels (hero treatment first) to the top of your Instagram profile.
- Keep Highlights for: Hero body treatment · Facials · HiFu · Brows · Reviews · Find us.
