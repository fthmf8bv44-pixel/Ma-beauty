# What we can and can't say about Thuzzle (and other devices) in Australia

Thuzzle is a radiofrequency medical device (made by GMV in Italy, distributed by The Global Beauty
Group). Distributors say it's on the ARTG (#385416). **That isn't verified yet**: check it on the TGA's ARTG
search or ask The Global Beauty Group. Advertising a therapeutic device to the public falls under the
**TGA Therapeutic Goods Advertising Code**, plus the **Australian Consumer Law** (no misleading claims)
and **Meta/TikTok ad policies**.

## ❌ Don't say
| Claim | Why not |
|---|---|
| "100% safe", "completely safe", "no side effects", "risk-free" | Banned in therapeutic advertising; no device is risk-free |
| "TGA approved" / "TGA registered" / "FDA approved" / "FDA cleared" | Ads can't imply government endorsement, and FDA status means nothing to Australian regulators and can mislead |
| "Only two clinics in Melbourne have Thuzzle" | Untrue: at least Melbourne Skin & Body (Bentleigh East) and Sculpt By J also list it (checked Nov 2026) |
| "Burns fat", "fat burning", "melts fat", "lose X cm" | Meta rejects these, and they need clinical evidence specific to the claim |
| "Permanent", "guaranteed", "instant results", "clinically proven" | Can't be substantiated for every client |
| "Tummy tuck" (without "non-surgical") | Implies surgery |
| Before/after photos in ads | Meta/TikTok policy (organic only, real, consented) |

## ✅ Snappy, compliant alternatives
- "Only a few Melbourne clinics have Thuzzle. We do."
- "Used by clinics around the world" (instead of "world-renowned", which is vaguer and harder to back up)
- "A non-surgical alternative: no surgery, no anaesthetic, no needles, no downtime" (instead of "safer alternative")
- "Thuzzle is still rare in Melbourne."
- "Italian-engineered radiofrequency."
- "Targets stubborn fat. Tightens skin."
- "Non-surgical. No needles. No downtime." (body; say "minimal downtime" for face resurfacing)
- "15–30 minutes per area."
- "Comfortable. Most clients describe a deep warmth." [CONFIRM with Mira]
- "A course gives the best results. Results vary."
- "From $159."
- "Performed by Mira, with decades of skin and body experience."
