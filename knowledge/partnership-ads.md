# Partnership ads (Meta) and Spark Ads (TikTok): plain-English guide

## What a partnership ad is
An ad that shows **both** names at the top, e.g. "*@localcreator* with *MA Beauty*". It runs from
your ad account (you pay and control targeting) but uses another person's post or identity.
People trust a real person more than a brand, so these ads often get cheaper clicks and bookings.

**Good partners for MA Beauty:** local micro-influencers (1k–20k followers in Caulfield, Bayside,
Glen Eira, Stonnington), happy clients who post about their treatment, nearby businesses
(pilates studio, hairdresser, bridal shop).

## How to set one up (Meta)
Menu names change sometimes. If something looks different, ask Claude.
1. **Agree terms first** (free treatment, payment or swap). Get it in writing, including how long
   you can run the ad (e.g. 3 months).
2. **The creator posts the content** on Instagram and turns on **"Add paid partnership label"**,
   choosing MA Beauty as the brand partner.
   *Or* the creator gives you a **partnership ad code**: Instagram → Settings → Creator tools and
   controls → Partnership ads → create a code → send it to you.
3. **You approve** the partnership request in Instagram (Settings → Business tools and
   controls → Branded content), if asked.
4. **In Meta Ads Manager:** create the campaign as normal → at the **Ad** level, switch on
   **Partnership ad** → choose the creator's post (or paste the code) → add your headline, CTA
   (Book now) and Fresha link → publish.
5. Use the same targeting as your other ads (5 km, 18+). Partnership ads can also reach the
   creator's followers.

## TikTok Spark Ads (TikTok's version)
1. Creator opens their TikTok video → … → **Ad settings** → turn on → **generate authorisation
   code** (choose 30/60/365 days) → sends it to you.
2. TikTok Ads Manager → Ad → **Use TikTok account to deliver Spark Ads** → paste the code.
3. You can also Spark-boost **your own** TikToks the same way.

## Rules you must follow
- Paid or gifted content must be clearly disclosed (the paid partnership label or #ad). This is
  required in Australia under the AANA Code and ACCC influencer guidance.
- Treatment claims must be honest and typical, with no "permanent" or guaranteed results and no
  before/after images in the ad. Target 18+ for cosmetic treatments.
- Get written permission before using any client's face or results.
