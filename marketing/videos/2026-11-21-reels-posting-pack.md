## How to save the videos to your phone
Tap each video in the chat → **Save** (or **Save to Photos**). The cover images save the same way.

## Sound (important)
The Reels are exported **without music**, so you can add a trending sound in Instagram, which helps reach.
- When posting: after choosing the video, tap **Audio** (music note) → pick a sound from the
  **Trending** list (an upward arrow ↗ means it's trending). Choose something calm and upbeat, not lyric-heavy.
- Trim the sound so the best part plays over the first 3 seconds → **Done**.
- Make sure **"Original audio" volume is 0** (the clips have background noise).

## How to post a Reel on Instagram (same steps for all three)
1. Instagram → **+** → **Reel** → choose the video → **Next**.
2. Tap **Audio** and add a trending sound (see above) → **Next**.
3. **Edit cover** → **Add from camera roll** → choose the matching cover image (`cover-*.jpg`). Check it
   looks right in the square grid preview.
4. Paste the **caption** and **hashtags** below.
5. **Tag location** → MA Beauty (or Caulfield South, Victoria).
6. **Share to Facebook**: ON (also Recommend on Facebook: ON).
7. **More options → Accessibility → Alt text**: paste the alt text.
8. **Share**.
9. **First hour:** reply to every comment, and share the Reel to your Story with a **Link sticker**
   (Fresha link) and the text "Book via the link".
10. **Pin it** to your profile if it's Reel 1 (tap ⋯ → Pin to your profile).

**TikTok** (once you set it up): open TikTok → **+** → **Upload** → choose the same video → add a sound from
TikTok's library → paste the TikTok caption → **Post**. Upload the file directly. Never share the
Instagram link to TikTok.
