Create a paid ad campaign for MA Beauty. Request: $ARGUMENTS

1. If the service, budget or platform isn't clear from the request, ask the owner (keep it to one
   question with options). Defaults: Meta, $10–$15/day, 14 days.
2. Check `business/profile.md`. If the service price or offer is marked [CONFIRM], ask the owner to
   confirm it before writing ads around it.
3. Use the **ads-manager** agent to produce the plan: goal, audience, budget and schedule, a full
   click-by-click build sheet for Ads Manager, 3–4 ad variations (primary text, headline,
   description, CTA), creative briefs, and what success looks like after 7 and 14 days.
4. Use the **graphic-designer** agent to create the ad creatives in 1080×1350 and 1080×1920.
5. Run the compliance checklist from the ads-manager agent and show it ticked.
6. Save to `marketing/campaigns/YYYY-MM-<name>.md` and give the owner a short summary with the
   build steps and the PNG locations.

Never claim the campaign is live. The owner publishes it.
