## Colours
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Soft porcelain | `#F7F1EC` |
| Primary | Rose clay | `#B76E5D` |
| Dark text | Espresso | `#2E2320` |
| Accent | Champagne gold | `#C9A66B` |
| Light accent | Blush | `#EBD3CB` |

## Fonts
- Headings: **Cormorant Garamond** (elegant serif), with Georgia as fallback
- Body and prices: **Montserrat** (clean sans-serif), with Helvetica/Arial as fallback

## Tone of voice
- Warm, calm, expert, like a trusted local skin therapist. Not salesy or pushy.
- Short sentences. Use "you" and "your skin."
- Positive framing: "glow", "refresh", "lift", "confidence", "self-care".
- Emojis: 0–2 per caption, and only where the owner would naturally use one. Few in ads.
- Australian English.

## Photo and video style
- Natural daylight, clean clinic shots, close-ups of glowing skin, hands at work, product textures.
- Real clients (with written consent) and the real clinic beat stock photos.
- Reels and TikToks: treatment process, "come with me to my facial", day-in-the-clinic, quick
  skin tips, meet the therapist.

## Graphic sizes

| Square feed post / carousel | 1080 × 1080 |
