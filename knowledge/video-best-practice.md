# Short-form video best practice (Reels, TikTok, Stories, video ads)

## Format
- 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, H.264 MP4. Loudness normalised (the editor does this).
- **Safe zone:** keep text and key action out of the top ~220 px (account name) and bottom ~420 px
  (caption, buttons), and away from the right-hand ~140 px (like/comment/share icons).
  Put text roughly between y = 300 and y = 1400, x = 80 to 920.
- The Instagram profile grid shows a **3:4 crop** (1080×1440 from the centre). Choose covers that
  still work cropped.

## Length
| Purpose | Length |
|---|---|
| Satisfying treatment clip / loop | 6–12 s |
| Reel or TikTok (process, results, day in the clinic) | 15–30 s |
| Education ("What is HiFu?", aftercare) | 30–60 s |
| Meta / TikTok ad | 9–20 s (make a 6 s cut too for stories) |

## The hook (first 1–2 seconds decides everything)
- Open on movement or the most striking shot, never a logo or a slow pan of the room.
- Pair it with a short text hook. Formats that work for clinics:
  - "The body treatment nobody else in Melbourne is doing"
  - "POV: your first [treatment] at MA Beauty"
  - "What [treatment] actually feels like"
  - "3 things I tell every client before HiFu"
  - "Is [treatment] worth it? Honest answer from your therapist"
  - "Come with me to my 10am client"
- Talking to camera (the owner or therapist) builds trust fast and works very well in ads.

## Pace and style
- Cuts every 1–3 s. Remove pauses and "ums". Slight speed-up (1.2–1.5×) on slow process shots.
- Raw, real and well lit beats polished and corporate. Phone footage is fine, and often better.
- Captions on: most people watch without sound.
- End with a clear next step: "Book via link in bio", "Free consult. DM 'BODY'".

## Sound
- Organic: add a trending sound inside Instagram/TikTok when posting (helps reach). Keep original
  audio if someone is speaking.
- Ads: only licensed or royalty-free audio (Meta Sound Collection, TikTok Commercial Music Library).

## Ideas that suit MA Beauty footage
- Treatment ASMR close-ups (device on skin, product textures)
- "What happens at your free consultation"
- Client reaction right after (with consent)
- Myth vs fact about the hero body treatment
- Meet the therapist / why I started MA Beauty
- Behind the scenes: setting up the room on Hawthorn Road
