# Don't look or sound AI-made (checklist)

The owner has asked that nothing looks like it came from AI. Check every graphic, caption and ad
against this list before showing it, and fix every hit.

## Visual giveaways to avoid
- Decorative blobs, arches, orbs, rings, wavy lines or floating shapes added "for interest"
- Soft gradient backgrounds, glows, sparkles, bokeh, glassmorphism
- Pill-shaped "BOOK NOW" buttons that look like a website (it's an image, so nobody can tap it).
  Use plain text like "Book via link in bio" instead
- Wide letter-spaced capitals on several lines (one small label at most)
- Elegant serif headline + thin sans subhead + gold line: the default "luxury beauty AI" look
- Perfect centred symmetry on every design, every element boxed in
- AI-generated or stock images of people/skin. **Only real MA Beauty photos and footage**
- Too much text. A real clinic post is mostly photo with a few words
- The same layout on every post

## What real, on-brand clinic content looks like
- A real photo or video still filling the frame (the clinic, the therapist, the device, real skin)
- A few words in the brand font, placed where the photo has space (not always the same place)
- The real logo, small, or no logo at all on organic posts
- Imperfect and personal: the owner's own phrasing, real prices, real reviews
- Consistency with the website and Instagram feed already out there

## Copy giveaways to avoid
- Em dashes (—). Use full stops or commas
- Words: elevate, indulge, unlock, unleash, transform(ative), radiant (overused), glow up your…,
  pamper, nestled, curated, bespoke (unless the owner uses it), journey, game-changer, holistic,
  "Say goodbye to…", "Look no further", "Discover the secret", "treat yourself", "you deserve"
- Three-adjective lists ("smooth, firm and radiant") and rhetorical question + answer openers
- Emoji bullet lists (✨ … 💆‍♀️ … 🌿 …) and more than 2 emojis
- Hashtag walls in the middle of captions (put them at the end, or in the first comment)
- Generic claims that could be about any salon

## Instead
- Short, specific, conversational. Mention the actual treatment, how it feels, the price, the
  street, the therapist's name. Write like the owner texting a regular client.
