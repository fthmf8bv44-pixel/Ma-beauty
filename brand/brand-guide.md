# MA Beauty: Brand Guide

Matched to mabeautytherapy.com.au (November 2026). Instagram (@mabeautyau) couldn't be viewed
from here because it needs a login. If the feed looks different, send screenshots and update this file.
Also follow `knowledge/no-ai-look.md`.

## Logo
- `brand/logo-black.png` and `brand/logo-white.png` (transparent PNG): "MA" monogram inside an open
  circle, with "BEAUTY THERAPY & COSMETIC TATTOO" curving around it.
- White logo on oxblood or on dark photos, black logo on white or grey.
- The clinic wall has a **gold** version of the logo (seen behind Mira in photos).

## Colours
| Role | Hex | Where it's used on the website |
|---|---|---|
| **Oxblood** (primary) | `#370C0E` | Header, footer, large colour blocks |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds, text on oxblood |
| Soft grey | `#E8E6E6` | Alternate blocks behind photos |
| Warm gold (accent, sparing) | `#B8955A` | Logo on the clinic wall. Use it for thin details only |
| Body text grey | `#595959` | Small body copy |

## Type (from the website)
- **Headings:** *Branch* (`brand/fonts/ma-heading.woff2`), a thin, high-contrast serif. Sentence or
  title case, never all caps. Used large.
- **Labels and buttons:** Montserrat, small, **uppercase with wide letter-spacing** (e.g. "WELCOME TO",
  "BOOK YOUR APPOINTMENT"). This is part of the real brand, so use it for one small label per design.
- **Body:** Helvetica Neue Light (fallback Helvetica/Arial).
- Load the heading font in HTML with:
  `@font-face{font-family:Branch;src:url('<relative path>/brand/fonts/ma-heading.woff2') format('woff2')}`

## Layout feel (website)
- Big split blocks: a photo on one half and oxblood with centred white text on the other.
- Lots of white space, centred text, thin outlined buttons.
- Real professional photography of the clinic and of Mira at work (no illustrations).

## Tone of voice
- Warm, personal, expert, honest. Mira talking to a client she's known for years.
- Clients love that she's "not sales-y", so never sound pushy.
- Short sentences. Australian English. 0–2 emojis per caption (the reviews use 🤍 ❤️ 😊).
- Name Mira. People book Mira, not a brand.
