# MA Beauty: Marketing Plan

## Goal
Get more **local** people to discover MA Beauty and book through Fresha (or book a free consult).

## Who we're for (starting assumptions: refine with the owner)
1. **Skin-focused women aged 28–55** living within about 5 km who want glowing, healthy skin
   (facials, LED, Korean Glass Skin).
2. **Anti-ageing, non-surgical clients aged 35–60** interested in lifting and tightening
   without injectables or surgery (HiFu).
3. **Brow and eyeliner clients aged 25–55** who want low-maintenance makeup (cosmetic tattoo).
4. **Gift buyers** (partners, family) around Mother's Day, Christmas and birthdays (vouchers).

## Local area (ad targeting radius: about 5 km around 342 Hawthorn Rd)
Caulfield South, Caulfield, Caulfield North, Caulfield East, Elsternwick, Glen Huntly,
Carnegie, Bentleigh, Bentleigh East, McKinnon, Ormond, Brighton East, Murrumbeena, St Kilda East.

## Starting ad budget (recommended)
- **Start: about $10–$15/day ($300–$450/month) on Meta.** Enough for a small local audience,
  and you'll learn what works within 2–3 weeks.
- Split: ~70% on one "hero" offer campaign (e.g. Korean Glass Skin Facial or a HiFu offer),
  ~30% on retargeting people who engaged with your Instagram/Facebook.
- Review every 2 weeks. Scale what brings bookings, and pause what doesn't.
- TikTok Ads: hold until organic TikTok videos show traction (minimum ~$20/day ad group there).
